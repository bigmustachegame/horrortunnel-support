SwarmThem Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

SwarmThem is an internal publishing tool operated by Big Mustache Games. It publishes short videos that Big Mustache Games made about its own apps and games to social media accounts owned by Big Mustache Games.

What data we process

  • Access tokens for our own accounts, issued by the platform after we authorize the tool. They are stored only on our own computer.
  • Basic profile information and statistics of our own posts (for example views and likes), used to measure our own marketing.

What we do not do

SwarmThem is not offered to the public. It does not log in other users, does not collect personal data of other people, and does not sell or share data with third parties.

Deletion

We can revoke the tool's access at any time in the platform settings. Tokens are then deleted from our computer.
